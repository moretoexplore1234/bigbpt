About Big B PT

BigB Personal Training offers a uniquely engaging and motivating angle to personal training. BigB (Baruch Meiselman) has set a name for himself in the Lakewood/Toms River area as a trustworthy and high quality PT, blending his own personal fitness journey experiences with his keen sense and ability to assess and internalize his clients' needs and goals, while professionally improving his clients' weaknesses and maximizing their strengths.

From losing 75 lb at the age of 15, to navigating a debilitating and lingering chronic knee injury, to playing semi-professional tackle football, Baruch knows the trials and tribulations a fitness journey can put you through, and applies his personal experiences to what his clients are going through.

In short, Baruch offers a unique training perspective and style unparalleled in the Frum world. Now located in the Five Towns area, BigBPT is bringing his A game to Long Island, but is selling out slots fast. Call him at +1-848-367-9672 to find a program and a rate that works for you!
